## Why Convert Word to PDF?

PDF files look the same on every device. Word documents don't.

When you share a `.docx` file, the recipient's version of Word might show:
- Different fonts
- Broken line breaks
- Missing images
- Shifted page layouts

PDF fixes all of this. Every reader sees the same document, pixel-for-pixel.

## The Problem With Most Converters

Many online converters mess up the formatting. They:
- Replace your fonts with generic ones
- Break tables
- Move images
- Add watermarks

A good converter keeps **everything intact**.

## What About Formatting?

Modern PDF converters preserve:
- Fonts (including custom ones)
- Tables and borders
- Images and positioning
- Headers and footers
- Page numbers
- Bullet points and numbering

The only things that might change:
- Very complex macros (not supported in PDF anyway)
- Embedded audio/video (PDF can't play them)

## Common Issues and Fixes

**Issue:** Fonts look different in the PDF.
**Fix:** Embed fonts in your Word document first (File → Options → Save → Embed fonts).

**Issue:** Images are missing.
**Fix:** Make sure images are embedded (not linked) in Word.

**Issue:** Tables break across pages.
**Fix:** Use "Keep with next" for table rows in Word.
