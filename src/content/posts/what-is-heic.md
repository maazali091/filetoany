## What is HEIC?

HEIC stands for **High Efficiency Image Container**. It's the default photo format on iPhones running iOS 11 and later.

The main reason Apple switched to HEIC is **file size**. A HEIC photo takes up about **50% less space** than the same photo saved as JPG — with no visible loss in quality.

## Why HEIC Causes Problems

HEIC isn't universally supported. Windows, older Android phones, and many websites can't open HEIC files directly.

If you've ever tried to upload an iPhone photo to a website and got an error, that's why.
